Important notes

This app is a helper only. It does not replace official charts, a marine compass, VHF radio, GPS plotter, navigation training, or local boating rules.

Compass sensors in phones can be affected by metal, speakers, magnets, engines, wiring, and the phone case. Use the correction controls only after comparing with a known direction or reliable compass.

Compass correction

If the heading appears about 90° to the east/off, press Apply -90° correction. If it goes the wrong way, press Apply +90° correction. Fine tune with ±5°.
